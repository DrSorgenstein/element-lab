Visual guide · count the atoms

Each square stands for one atom. Spaces divide long rows into groups of five. The rows show the counts in one written formula.

A molecular formula counts atoms in one molecule. In ionic solids such as table salt, a formula gives the ratio of ions in the crystal. A number after parentheses multiplies every atom inside. A missing subscript means one. A molecular formula gives composition. Different arrangements of the same atoms can have different properties. This tray models atom counts, not a reaction or a bond structure.
